FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I generate quotes on remote job sites without cell service?

Yes. Vanza Docs is built strictly offline-first. Your entire item catalog, client address book, calculations, and PDF generation work with zero internet. You only need network connection when sending the final PDF via Telegram, WhatsApp, or Email.

Where is my financial and client data stored?

All your data is saved exclusively on your phone's internal storage via IndexedDB. We do not operate external database servers or track your revenue. For safety, you can optionally enable automated backups to your personal Google Drive.

Do generated documents hold legal status?

Yes. Generated PDFs contain all required commercial requisites (tax IDs, IBAN bank numbers, legal entity names, detailed scope of work, sum written in words), along with graphic client signatures and company seals.
