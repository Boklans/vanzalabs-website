FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Muscle Recovery Engine calculate readiness?

SPOT models muscle fatigue based on volume, intensity, and time-decay curves. Different muscle groups have different recovery windows (e.g., small muscle groups recover in ~36–48h while spinal erectors and quads take longer). SPOT estimates this to help you pick the best day for each split.

Does SPOT work without an internet connection?

Yes. Gym basements frequently have zero mobile signal. SPOT stores your workout routines, logs, and timer locally on device so you never lose a set.

Is my workout data private?

Completely. We do not sell your biometric, health, or workout data to any advertisers or third-party brokers.
