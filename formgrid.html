Core Architecture

Engineered for field work. Zero web browser friction.

Google Forms and Sheets on mobile browsers are slow and clumsy. FormGrid gives you a fast, native workspace with offline queues and instant QR generation.

1-Tap Form & Sheet Studio

Build custom Google Forms (short text, checkboxes, dropdowns, radio options) with an automatic linked Google Spreadsheet generated in a single tap without touching a browser.

  • ✓ Pre-built business templates for orders, attendance, and inventory
  • ✓ Instant direct shareable link for clients and respondents

Offline Submission Queue

Collect responses in warehouses, basements, or remote job sites with zero cell reception. Submissions are safely buffered locally and auto-synced the second connection returns.

  • ✓ Never lose a client lead or checklist entry due to dropped connection
  • ✓ Background auto-retry mechanism without manual intervention

Direct Client-to-Google Privacy

FormGrid talks strictly between your phone and official Google Drive/Sheets APIs. Your confidential business forms and sheet entries never pass through any middleman servers.

  • ✓ Secure official Google Sign-In with OAuth 2.0 verification
  • ✓ Restricted drive.file scope — cannot inspect or read your private drive

Dynamic QR Code Studio & Share

Generate publication-ready vector QR codes for your forms and spreadsheets instantly. Print for event check-ins, customer feedback stands, or restaurant menus in seconds.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can FormGrid access my other private files on Google Drive?

No. FormGrid exclusively requests the strictest `drive.file` scope. By design, Google only allows the application to access the specific files it created itself. Your private photos, emails, and other personal Drive files remain completely inaccessible.

How does the offline submission queue work?

When collecting field survey responses or inventory data without cell reception, FormGrid safely buffers each submission in local storage. As soon as your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, all entries are automatically synchronized to Google Sheets.

Can I use FormGrid for free?

Yes. FormGrid provides a generous free tier allowing you to manage up to 5 full documents with all core features enabled. For unlimited workspaces and advanced tools, you can upgrade via lifetime access or a flexible subscription with a 7-day free trial.
